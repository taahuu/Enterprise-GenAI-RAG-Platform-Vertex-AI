# Northwind Enterprises – Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 10 unused days may be carried forward into the next year; any days above that limit expire on 31 March. Planned leave must be requested at least 2 weeks in advance through the PeopleHub portal and is approved by the employee's line manager.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required when sick leave lasts more than 3 consecutive days. Employees must notify their manager before the start of their working day on the first day of absence.

## Parental leave
The primary caregiver is entitled to 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver is entitled to 12 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave can be taken in one block or split into two blocks within the first 18 months after the birth or adoption.

## Public holidays and bereavement
Northwind observes the public holidays of the country in which the employee is contracted. Employees may take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member.
