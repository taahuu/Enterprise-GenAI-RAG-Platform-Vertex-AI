# Northwind Enterprises – Travel and Expense Policy

## Submitting expenses
Expense claims must be submitted in the SpendDesk app within 30 days of the expense date. A receipt is required for every expense above $25. Claims submitted after 30 days may be rejected.

## Approval thresholds
Claims up to $1,000 are approved by the line manager. Claims above $1,000 also require approval from the department director. Reimbursement is paid with the next monthly payroll run.

## Travel
Flights shorter than 6 hours must be booked in economy class. Flights of 6 hours or longer may be booked in premium economy. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to $220 per night. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to $60 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Ground transport
Use public transport or ride-share for airport transfers. Car rental requires prior approval from the line manager, and mileage for personal vehicles is reimbursed at $0.60 per mile.
