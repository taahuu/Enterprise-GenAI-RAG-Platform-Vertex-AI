Northwind Insight – Product FAQ

What plans does Northwind Insight offer?
Northwind Insight is offered in three plans. Starter costs $49 per user per month, Business costs $99 per user per month, and Enterprise is priced by custom quote.

Does Northwind Insight support single sign-on?
SAML single sign-on (SSO) is available on the Business and Enterprise plans. The Starter plan supports email and password login only.

How long is data retained?
The Starter plan retains dashboard data for 13 months and the Business plan retains it for 36 months. Enterprise customers can configure a custom retention period.

What are the API rate limits?
The Starter plan allows 100 API requests per minute. The Business plan allows 1,000 requests per minute. Requests above the limit receive an HTTP 429 response.

What is the uptime guarantee?
Business and Enterprise plans are covered by a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA. If uptime falls below the SLA, customers receive service credits.

How do I contact support?
Starter customers get email support on business days. Business customers get 24x7 chat support. Enterprise customers get a named technical account manager.
