# Northwind Enterprises – IT Security Policy

## Passwords and authentication
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all corporate accounts, including email, VPN and the cloud console. Passwords are rotated only when a compromise is suspected; routine forced rotation is not required.

## Devices
All company laptops use full-disk encryption and are managed through the Company Portal. Employees may not install software outside the Company Portal. USB mass-storage devices are blocked by default; exceptions require written approval from the security team.

## Lost or stolen devices
A lost or stolen device must be reported to security@northwind.example within 1 hour of discovery. The security team will remotely lock and wipe the device.

## Remote access
The corporate VPN is required whenever an employee connects to internal systems from a public or untrusted network such as a café, airport or hotel Wi-Fi.

## Data classification
Company data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted data, such as customer payment details and employee health records, must be encrypted at rest and in transit and may only be shared with named individuals.
